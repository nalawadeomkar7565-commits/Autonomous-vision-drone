Machine Vision Drone: YOLOv8n vs YOLOv8s

Document Overview

This specification document traces the high-level product requirements (identifying a person, tracking them in unstructured environments, and performing obstacle avoidance) down through the software architecture and finally to the physical hardware constraints. It explicitly compares the lightweight YOLOv8n (Nano) model against the slightly heavier, more accurate YOLOv8s (Small) model running on a Raspberry Pi 5 paired with a Hailo-8L NPU.

1 Product Requirements (Functional)

Feature YOLOv8n (Nano) YOLOv8s (Small) - Recommended
Tracking Distance Short-Range (2–5m): Loses the lock on a person if they walk too far away or become a small cluster of pixels. Medium/Long-Range (5–15m+): Maintains a solid lock on the identified person even at a distance.
Environmental Robustness Low: Struggles heavily with shadows, dusk, or partial occlusions (e.g., walking behind a tree branch). High: Highly capable in natural, unlit outdoor environments due to its deeper mathematical network.
Tracking Reactivity Extremely Fast: Hyper-reactive to sudden, jerky movements. Fast: Smooth and reliable, perfect for cinematic tracking or standard follow-me modes.
Palm Landing Accuracy Rapid response to sudden hand movements at close range. Slower response to sudden jerks, but higher confidence in identifying a palm correctly.

2 System Architecture & Software Specs

Specification YOLOv8n (Nano) YOLOv8s (Small)
Mathematical Load ~8.7 Billion FLOPs per frame. ~28.6 Billion FLOPs per frame.
Expected FPS (Hailo-8L) ~120+ FPS: Leaves massive overhead for the NPU. ~60 to 80 FPS: Maxes out NPU capabilities, well above the 30 FPS flight control threshold.
Flight Control Latency Near-zero latency. CPU has abundant free time to process obstacle detection. Slightly higher processing latency, but delivers MAVLink commands well within safe limits.
RAM Requirement 4GB RAM is sufficient. 4GB RAM is sufficient.

3 Hardware Specifications & Physical Requirements

Hardware Component YOLOv8n (Nano) YOLOv8s (Small)
Thermal Management (Pi 5) Standard active cooler required. Standard active cooler required.
Thermal Management (NPU) Basic passive heatsink on the Hailo-8L chip is sufficient. Mandatory Active Cooling
The Hailo-8L will run hot under continuous 28 GFLOP load. Must use a dedicated fan or position in propeller downwash.
Power Consumption ~7W to 8W total system draw. ~10W to 12W total system draw under continuous heavy inference.
Power Delivery (BEC) Standard 5V/3A BEC likely survives, though 5A is safer. Strict Requirement
Requires a high-quality 5V/5A (25W) dedicated BEC. The NPU causes rapid power spikes. A weak BEC will cause voltage brownouts and mid-air crashes.
Payload Weight Lighter (smaller heatsinks, standard power modules). Slightly heavier (larger heatsinks/fans, heavy-duty power modules).

Design Summary

By choosing YOLOv8s to ensure the drone can robustly follow a locked person in natural environments, your software and RAM requirements remain virtually unchanged from the Nano model. However, your hardware requirements strictly increase regarding thermal cooling (adding fans/airflow) and power delivery (mandating a high-quality 5V/5A BEC).
